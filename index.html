Overview

Product thinking, backed by a design eye

I'm an experienced product manager with a strong background in Agile product development, roadmap planning, and functional specification writing — paired with hands-on skill in UI/UX and graphic design, data visualization, and presentation design . That combination means I can define what should be built, help design what it looks like, and communicate why it matters to stakeholders. My work spans B2B agri-tech supply chains, telecom and FMCG brand campaigns, B2B2C e-commerce, SaaS platforms, and freelance brand identity — always aimed at user-centered outcomes that also move business metrics.

How I work

My product thinking process

The workflow I run for every product, from a first messy requirement to a shipped release.

01 · Discover

Understand the problem

Dig into stakeholder needs, existing workflows, and market context before defining scope — turning ambiguous asks into a clear problem statement.

02 · Define

Roadmap & requirements

Translate discovery into prioritized roadmaps and functional specs — writing clear requirements and user stories in JIRA that engineering and design can build from.

03 · Deliver

Agile execution

Run sprints and scrum ceremonies, coordinating product, design, and engineering so the team ships aligned, on schedule, and without surprises.

04 · Validate

UAT & iterate

Lead UAT, surface gaps before launch, and use data and stakeholder feedback to shape the next iteration.

I lean on AI-augmented workflows (ChatGPT, Claude) throughout this process — to accelerate research, sharpen requirements, and speed up UX/UI concepting.
